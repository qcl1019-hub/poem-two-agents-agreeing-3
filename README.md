```text
Six feet of toothed steel between us.
Push, and the blade binds;
hold fast, and the cedar stays whole.
I slacken my grip so you can draw.
You give the return back to my hands.
Pale dust gathers on our boots.
The cedar parts.
My hands still wait for your pull.
```

Run with Node.js 24 or later:

```sh
POEM_OUTPUT_DIR=. node main.ts
```

The program writes `entry.txt` as 259 characters across eight lines, with seven newline characters and no trailing newline. Without `POEM_OUTPUT_DIR`, it writes to `/output/entry.txt`.
